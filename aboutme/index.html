My Story

Hi, I'm Elliott, a game designer currently studying at UC Irvine pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Video Game Design.


I grew up in San Diego surrounded by a family of gamers, but my true obsession with game development started when I was playing Fat Princess and my dad pointed out his own name in the credits. From that moment on, I knew I wanted to create experiences that would put my name on the screen and bring joy to others.
