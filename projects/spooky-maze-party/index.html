Spooky Maze Party

About This Project

Currently being developed by Sidewalk Snail Games during my internship with them, Spooky Maze Party is a third person party game where players are dropped into a haunted maze and must outwit and outrun roaming ghosts to find their way out. With unpredictable layouts and extremely frantic chases, each round keeps players on edge while still being lighthearted and cute fun for friends.
