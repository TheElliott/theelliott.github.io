Till Theft Do Us Part

About This Project

Made solo for the 2-day IGDA San Diego Game Jam 2025

You play as Sparks and Steel, a loving couple sneaking their way through a museum to steal paintings. With teamwork, clever puzzles, and lighthearted banter, you'll push boxes, dodge patrols, and outsmart obstacles to steal the paintings and escape. It's a mix of stealth and puzzle-solving, wrapped in romance.
